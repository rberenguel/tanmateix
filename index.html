About Tanmateix

Tanmateix is a logic puzzle game where you evaluate whether conclusions logically follow from given premises.

How to Play

You'll be presented with statements (premises) about entities and their relationships. Your task is to determine if the conclusion logically follows from the premises by selecting True or False.

Example

Premise 1: A is larger than B
Premise 2: B is larger than C
Conclusion: A is larger than C? True

Train your logical reasoning skills through 10 questions per game!
